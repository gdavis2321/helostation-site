From above

Tucked away in the country, minutes from Fort Worth

The Helo Station feels like the country but is only minutes southwest of downtown Fort Worth. It sits on 6 private, tree-lined acres at the end of a quiet cul-de-sac. A gravel driveway winds up to the hangar, with grass parking along the way and open fields all around. That's where the sunsets come from.

The namesake

Yes, there's a real helicopter

The Helo Station is home to a working helicopter. When it's on site, it makes a one-of-a-kind backdrop for your photos. For everyone's safety, we ask guests to admire it from a distance and keep the big driveway clear.

How booking works

Simple from first call to cleanup

  1. Tell us your date

    Send your date, headcount and type of event. We'll confirm availability and give you a quote.

  2. Get your event guide

    You'll receive a personal event guide with your fees, what's included, and everything you need to know.

  3. Set up

    Rental companies and vendors may deliver the day before your event, or two days ahead on request.

  4. Celebrate & wrap up

    Enjoy your event. Teardown is due by the end of the next day, or Monday afternoon for Sunday events.

Payment is due in full on or before your event day. We accept Venmo, Zelle and Apple Cash. Tables, chairs and trash removal can be included or added as low-cost add-ons. The outdoor tent is available for an extra structure fee.
